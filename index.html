BoardScore

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

BoardScore ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application for managing your board game collection and tracking game session results. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

By using BoardScore, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Category Data Purpose
Account Email address, display name Account creation and identification
Game library Games added to collection, favorites Core app functionality
Session history Game played, player names, scores/places, date, notes Score tracking and history
Purchases Subscription status (Pro tier) Unlocking premium features
App preferences Language, dark mode, onboarding status Personalization, stored locally on device

We do not collect precise location, photos, contacts, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing and maintaining the BoardScore service
  • Syncing your game collection and session history across devices
  • Verifying your subscription status to unlock Pro features
  • Enabling anonymous use (guest accounts with limited features)
  • Diagnosing errors and improving app stability

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

Supabase
We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure (authentication, database, and real-time sync). Your data is stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database with row-level security — only you can access your own data. Supabase Privacy Policy: supabase.com/privacy

RevenueCat
We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and verify purchase entitlements. RevenueCat receives your anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt data. It does not receive your email or name. RevenueCat Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

BoardGameGeek (BGG)
Game data (names, descriptions, images, ratings) is sourced from the BoardGameGeek public API and catalog. We do not send any personal data to BGG.

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Upon deletion, all your data (game library, session history, profile) is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

App preferences stored locally on your device (language, dark mode) are removed when you uninstall the app.

5. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Database access is protected by row-level security policies — your data can only be accessed by your authenticated user ID. We do not store passwords; authentication is handled by Supabase Auth (email/password with secure hashing, or anonymous sign-in).

6. Children's Privacy

BoardScore is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your display name from Settings
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data from Settings
  • Portability — request an export of your session history

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
